## Limitations

- Nine notes, hand-labelled by me after reading them and the mock's answers (`evals/gold.json`). A difference of one or two proposals is within noise; nothing here is statistically significant.
- The few-shot examples are new (none of N01–N09), but the prompt's rules were written after reading the nine notes, so both real-model conditions may be flattered. A held-out note set would be needed to separate prompt quality from prompt fitting.
- One model, one provider, one prompt version, temperature 0. The reasoning model is not deterministic, so repeated runs differ; results may change when the provider updates the model.
- Text fields (service notes) are scored on field only, not wording.
